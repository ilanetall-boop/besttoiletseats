Frequently Asked Questions

Will a silver elongated toilet seat fit any elongated toilet bowl?

Yes, as long as your bowl is actually elongated and not round. Elongated bowls measure about 18.5 inches from the mounting bolts to the front edge, versus roughly 16.5 inches for round bowls. Finish, including the brushed silver on the BAHZETY seat, does not change the mounting hardware or bolt spacing.

Does the brushed silver finish scratch or fade over time?

Verified owner reviews describe the finish holding up well against normal wiping and standard bathroom cleaners. The coating is a top layer rather than solid metal, so cleaning with abrasive pads or heavy-duty scrubbers wears it down faster than gentle cleaning does.

Is this elongated toilet seat silver review pick worth it over a plain white seat?

If you have already updated your faucet, towel bar, or cabinet hardware to brushed nickel or chrome, the BAHZETY seat closes that visual gap for about the same price as many plain white elongated seats. If matching finish is not a priority, the Hibbent padded seat or Kohler ReadyLatch model may serve you better for less money or sturdier hinges.
