What Could Be Better

The wood core inside the Hibbent's plastic shell is the one honest drawback worth flagging. Wood resists cracking better than thin plastic alone, but it is more susceptible to swelling in a consistently humid bathroom than the fully molded plastic designs some competitors use, and a few reviewers mention this as a long-term watch item rather than a day-one problem.

Price is the other factor to weigh. At $53.99, the Hibbent costs almost eight dollars more than the Mayfair Bennett, and for buyers who need nothing more than a basic elongated replacement without the quick-release hinge, that gap is hard to justify.

The listing is also light on precise seat dimensions and weight capacity, two details that matter if you have a tight bathroom layout or specific support requirements. Measure your current seat before ordering, and if the listing does not answer a question you have, treat that as a reason to ask the seller directly rather than guess.

Is the Hibbent Premium Elongated Toilet Seat worth the price over the Mayfair Bennett?

No. This is an elongated seat, built for elongated bowls only. Installing it on a round bowl will leave gaps and an unstable fit, so measure your bowl shape before ordering any of the seats in this comparison.
