Premium electric bidet seats. Full electric bidet seats with heated water and air drying handle the twisting problem beautifully, but they often run several hundred dollars and need a nearby outlet. Plug-in vanity night lights. Standard plug-in lights brighten a room, but they sit away from the bowl and still ask you to flip a switch. The motion-sensor and bowl-rim lights here put the light where you step and remove the reach, which serves a stiff back better at night.
