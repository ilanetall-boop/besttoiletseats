Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Bemis 7850TDG worth the extra cost over a residential seat?

It's worth it if your restroom sees heavy daily traffic, since Bemis builds the commercial-grade shell and open-front design to hold up longer than typical residential seats. For a low-traffic home bathroom, a residential slow-close seat like the Kohler 20110-96 usually offers better comfort for the price. Weigh the $45.49 price against how often the seat gets used and who's replacing it if it fails.

Does the Bemis 7850TDG fit a standard elongated toilet?

Yes. Bemis designed it for elongated bowls, which cover most toilets installed in the US since the early 2000s. Measure your bowl from the mounting bolts to the front edge to confirm before ordering, since round-bowl toilets need a different seat.

Why doesn't the Bemis 7850TDG have a soft-close hinge?

Commercial seats like the 7850TDG typically skip soft-close hardware to reduce the number of moving parts that can fail under heavy use. That trade-off gives facility managers a simpler, more repair-resistant hinge instead of cushioned comfort.
