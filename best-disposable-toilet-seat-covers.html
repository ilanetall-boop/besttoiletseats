Frequently Asked Questions

Are disposable toilet seat covers actually sanitary?
Disposable covers create a physical barrier between your skin and the seat, which addresses direct contact with a surface you did not clean yourself. They do not sterilize the seat underneath, so they work best as a convenience and comfort layer rather than a disinfectant. Reviewers consistently note that individually wrapped covers stay cleaner in a bag than loose, fan-folded ones.
How many disposable toilet seat covers should I carry?
For occasional travel, a compact 20- to 60-count pack like the Relyo or MP MOZZPAK options above covers most trips without adding bulk to a bag. If you use public restrooms daily or you're managing potty training, a 100-count pack such as the Traletry or SFOPORD options works out to a lower cost per cover and needs fewer reorders.
Can you flush disposable toilet seat covers?
Some brands, including the DISPOZIT pack on this list, market their covers as flushable, and reviewers back that up in practice. Others are meant to be thrown away. Check the specific packaging before flushing, and if you're on a septic system or older plumbing, disposing of the cover in the trash is the safer default regardless of what the label claims.
